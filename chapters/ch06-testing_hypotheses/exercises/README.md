# Chapter 06 | Testing Hypotheses | Data Exercises

My answers to the Chapter 6 data exercises from [Data Analysis for Business, Economics, and Policy](https://gabors-data-analysis.com/) by Gábor Békés and Gábor Kézdi.

1. `01-billion_prices_other_country`: online vs. offline prices in a country other than the USA.
2. `02-billion_prices_with_sales`: the USA case study again, now including sales and promotions.
3. `03-billion_prices_same_day_by_store`: same-day prices only, plus one test per store and multiple-testing corrections.
4. `04-sp500_loss_threshold`: the S&P 500 loss test with my own loss definition and tolerated frequency.
5. `05-hotels_europe_two_dates`: hotel prices on two dates in one city, and whether the two groups of hotels are comparable.
